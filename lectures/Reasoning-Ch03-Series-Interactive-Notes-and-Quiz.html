Alphabetical Positions (Left & Right) & Opposite Pairs (वर्णमाला स्थिति और विपरीत अक्षर)

Golden Formula: Opposite Position = 27 − Alphabet Position | Wrap-Around Rule: If Position > 26, subtract 26 (e.g., Z(26) + 6 = 32 → 32 − 26 = 6 = F).

1. Forward Movement (+n) →

Each letter moves forward in the alphabet. Wraps around after Z (26).

A → B → C → D → E (+1)
Z → A → B → C → D (+1 wrap)
2. Backward Movement (−n) ←

Each letter moves to previous letter. If negative/zero, add 26 (e.g. C(3) − 8 = 29 − 8 = 21 = U).

E → D → C → B → A (−1)
U → O → I → E → A (Reverse Vowels)

Kamal Sir's Golden Decision Tree

Small Gap → Addition / Subtraction (+ / −)

Check 1st level or 2nd level difference, prime gaps, or n²±1.

Big Gap → Multiplication / Division (× / ÷)

Look at the last two large terms first! E.g. 41 → 167 → 839 (×4+3, ×5+4).

Dip & Rise (e.g. 40, 21, 22, 34, 69) → Half-Multiplier

When 2nd term is ~half of 1st term, immediately test ×0.5 + 1, ×1 + 1, ×1.5 + 1...

Up-Down-Up-Down → Alternating / Twin Series

Check odd positions (1st, 3rd, 5th) and even positions (2nd, 4th, 6th) separately.
