🎯 10 Mandatory Triggers for 'THAT' (Page 16) & 6 Forbidden Nouns (Page 19)

Use Relative Pronoun 'THAT' (instead of Who/Whom/Which) after:
1. The only (the only creature that)
2. The same (the same man that)
3. The few / The little
4. Superlative Degree (-est / most)
5. All (All that glitters)
6. None (none that can help)
7. Such (such...that/as)
8. -Thing (something, everything, nothing, anything)
9. Living + Non-Living/Animal
10. It + be + Time/Place + that
🚫 6 Nouns that NEVER take a Possessive Adjective before them (Page 19):

Favour, Leave, Separation, Excuse, Mention, Pardon

• Your separation ➔ The separation from you
• Your favour / His favour ➔ The favour of you / him
• Your leave / Her pardon ❌
