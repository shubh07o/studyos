Power & Algebraic Transformation Catalog

1. Square & Cube Offset (n² ± k / n³ ± k)

When numbers are just above or below standard squares (4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100, 121, 144, 169, 196, 225, 256...) or cubes (8, 27, 64, 125, 216, 343, 512, 729, 1000...).

• 8² + 3 = 67
• 11² + 3 = 124
• 5³ + 5 = 130
2. Hybrid Transition (n² – 1 : n³ + 1)

The first number is related to a base's square, while the second corresponds to the same base's cube!

• 24 : 126 ➔ (5² – 1) : (5³ + 1)
• 63 : 513 ➔ (8² – 1) : (8³ + 1)
3. Triad Set Operations (a, b, c)

Always test the middle term as a function of outer terms:

• a × c = b (Outer Product)
• (a + c) ÷ 2 = b (Outer Mean)
• (a + c)² = b (Sum Square)
• a × k + c × m = b (Weighted Sum)
