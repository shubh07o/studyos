📐 Trigonometry & Heights

  • • Golden Identity: यदि sin θ + sin2θ = 1, तो cos2θ + cos4θ = 1
  • • Complementary Angles: यदि B + C = 90°, तो tan B · tan C = 1
  • • Astroid Elimination: यदि x = a cos3θ, y = b sin3θ, तो (x/a)2/3 + (y/b)2/3 = 1

🔢 Quadratic Equations & Polynomials

  • • Difference of Roots: |x1 − x2| = √b2 − 4aca
  • • Reciprocal Roots Condition: c = a (गुणनफल α · (1/α) = 1)
  • • Equal Roots Condition: D = b2 − 4ac = 0
