📜 Text of the Preamble of India
Adopted 26 Nov 1949
THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA • PREAMBLE

"WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA, having solemnly resolved to constitute India into a SOVEREIGN SOCIALIST SECULAR DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC and to secure to all its citizens:

JUSTICE, social, economic and political;
LIBERTY of thought, expression, belief, faith and worship;
EQUALITY of status and of opportunity; and to promote among them all;
FRATERNITY assuring the dignity of the individual and the unity and integrity of the Nation;

IN OUR CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY this twenty-sixth day of November, 1949, do HEREBY ADOPT, ENACT AND GIVE TO OURSELVES THIS CONSTITUTION."

* Note: The terms Socialist, Secular, and Integrity were added later by the 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976 (Trick: ISS / SIS).

🏛️ Essence & Compilation
Core Concept
  • Introductory Statement: States the reasons and guiding values/philosophy of the Constitution.
  • Summary or Essence: Embodies the soul and philosophy of the entire Indian Constitution.
  • Compiled at the End: It was enacted and made after the entire Constitution was already compiled and adopted.
  • Modified Objective Resolution: Based on the Objective Resolution introduced by J.L. Nehru on 13 Dec 1946 and unanimously approved on 22 Jan 1947.
🌍 Borrowed Elements & Artistry
Exam Favorites
  • Phrase 'We the People of India': Borrowed from the Constitution of the USA.
  • Idea of Preamble: Borrowed from USA (first country to begin with a Preamble).
  • Language of Preamble: Borrowed from the Constitution of Australia.
  • Illumination & Decoration: Designed by artists of Shantiniketan — Beohar Rammanohar Sinha (Jabalpur) and Nandalal Bose. Calligraphy by Prem Behari Narain Raizada.

👑 Sovereign
Nature

India is neither a dependency nor a dominion of any other nation. It is an independent state.

  • People have supreme right to make decisions on internal as well as external matters.
  • No external power can dictate to the government of India.
🤝 Socialist
Nature

Added by 42nd Amendment (1976). Derived from Russian Revolution (1917).

  • Core Idea: Wealth is generated socially and should be shared equally; government regulates ownership to reduce inequality.
  • Economic Models: Capitalistic (USA) vs. Socialist (Russia) vs. Mixed Economy (India).
🕊️ Secular
Nature

Added by 42nd Amendment (1976). Complete religious freedom without state bias.

  • Positive Secularism (India): State gives equal respect and protection to all religions (Sarva Dharma Sambhava) and can interfere to eliminate social evils (e.g. untouchability).
  • Negative Secularism (USA / Western): Complete strict wall of separation; zero state interference.
🗳️ Democratic & Republic
Nature
  • Democracy: System of 'Government by consent' — John Locke (Father of Liberalism, book: Two Treatises of Government 1689).
  • Abraham Lincoln: "Government of the people, by the people, for the people shall not perish from the Earth."
  • Republic: Head of state is elected (not hereditary). UK is a Constitutional Monarchy (non-republic), while India is a Republic.
📚 Deep Dive: Types of Socialism & Marxism
Conceptual Clarity
1. Radical Socialism

Ending private ownership of all businesses/wealth entirely and making everything shared equally to create a classless society.

2. Gandhian Socialism

Promotes non-violence, self-reliance (swadeshi), village-centered economy, and Decentralised Planning.

3. Nehruvian Socialism

Emphasizes state-controlled key heavy industries to achieve growth and equality; known as Mixed Socialism with Centralised Planning.

⚒️ Karl Marx's Ideology (Marxism):

• Developed with Friedrich Engels in 19th Century. Society split into Bourgeoisie (wealthy owners) and Proletariat (working class).

• Exploitation: Owners extract surplus value by underpaying workers for their hard labor.

• Famous Books: The Communist Manifesto (1848) & Das Kapital / Capital (1867).

⚖️ Direct Democracy vs Indirect Democracy
Forms of Governance
Feature Direct Democracy Indirect (Representative) Democracy
Definition Citizens directly participate in decision-making and vote on laws & policies themselves. Citizens elect representatives who make laws and govern on their behalf.
Devices Referendum, Initiative, Recall, Plebiscite. Parliamentary elections, Legislative votes.
Country Examples Switzerland, Uruguay, Latvia, Lithuania India, Canada, USA, UK
⚖️ 3 Types of Justice
Borrowed: USSR

Social, Economic, and Political Justice:

  • Social Justice: Equal treatment of all citizens without discrimination based on caste, color, race, religion, sex.
  • Economic Justice: Non-discrimination between people on basis of wealth, income, and property.
  • Political Justice: All citizens have equal political access and voice in government.
🗽 5 Forms of Liberty
Borrowed: France

Absence of restraints on activities of individuals:

  • 1. Thought
  • 2. Expression
  • 3. Belief
  • 4. Faith
  • 5. Worship
🟰 2 Types of Equality
Borrowed: France

Absence of special privilege to any section of society:

  • 1. Equality of Status: No artificial civic or legal hierarchy.
  • 2. Equality of Opportunity: Adequate opportunities for all individuals without distinction.
🤝 Fraternity & Dignity
Borrowed: France

A sense of common brotherhood:

  • Assures the dignity of the individual.
  • Assures the unity and integrity of the Nation ('integrity' added in 1976).
  • Promoted via Single Citizenship and Article 51A (Fundamental Duties).
🏛️ Supreme Court Jurisprudence on Preamble
Vital Case Laws
Case Name & Year Supreme Court Ruling Is Preamble Part of Constitution? Can it be Amended?
Berubari Union Case (1960) Preamble is an aid to interpretation and key to the minds of makers, but NOT a part of the Constitution. ❌ NO ❌ NO
Kesavananda Bharati Case (1973) Overruled Berubari case! SC held Preamble IS a part of the Constitution. It can be amended under Article 368 without destroying Basic Structure. ✅ YES ✅ YES (under Art 368)
LIC of India Case (1995) Reaffirmed Kesavananda Bharati ruling: Preamble is an integral part of the Constitution. ✅ YES (Integral) ✅ YES
Ashwini Kumar Upadhyay v. UOI (Nov 2024) Supreme Court rejected petition challenging insertion of "Socialist" and "Secular" into Preamble, upholding 42nd CAA 1976. ✅ Fully Valid Affirmed validity of 1976 amendment.
Enforceability Rule: The Preamble is Non-Justiciable (not enforceable in a court of law). It is neither a source of power to the legislature nor a restriction upon legislative powers.

Quick Cheat Sheet

📌 Berubari vs Kesavananda Bharati

Berubari (1960): Preamble is NOT a part of the Constitution & CANNOT be amended.
Kesavananda (1973): Overruled Berubari! Preamble IS a part of the Constitution & CAN be amended under Article 368 without altering the Basic Structure.

📌 USA vs Australia Borrowings

USA: Idea of Preamble & the opening phrase 'We the People of India'.
Australia: Specific Language and style of the Preamble (along with Concurrent List).

📌 USSR vs France Objectives

USSR (Russia): Ideals of Justice (Social, Economic, and Political).
France: Ideals of Liberty, Equality, Fraternity, and Republic.

📌 42nd Amendment 1976 (Trick: ISS / SIS)

Preamble has been amended ONLY ONCE in 1976. Added 3 words: Integrity, Socialist, Secular. Upheld in Nov 2024 by Supreme Court.

📌 Justiciability & Power

The Preamble is Non-Justiciable (not enforceable in courts). It is neither a source of power nor a limitation/prohibition on the legislature.
