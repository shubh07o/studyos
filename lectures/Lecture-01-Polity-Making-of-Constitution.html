📜 What is a Constitution & World Origin

EXAM ESSENTIAL

A Constitution lays out certain ideals that form the basis of the kind of country a nation aspires to be.

• Tradition of Written Constitution began in: USA (in 1787).
• Independence of USA: 4th July 1776.
• Core Nuance: While all democratic countries are likely to have a Constitution, it is not necessary that all countries having a Constitution are democratic!

Democratic Government: Power is held by the people and exercised through their elected representatives.

⚖️ 4 Major Forms of Government

1. Communist

Means of production fully controlled by government.
e.g., China

2. Oligarchic

Government ruled by a small, powerful few.
e.g., Russia, China

3. Monarchic

Ruled by a single monarch/king.
e.g., Saudi Arabia, UK

4. Totalitarian

Total, absolute control over lives of citizens.
e.g., North Korea

⏳ Chronology of Demand for Constituent Assembly

1928
Nehru Report (Early Draft): Prepared and submitted by the All Parties Conference at Lucknow, chaired by Motilal Nehru.
1934
First Official Idea: M. N. Roy (pioneer of communist movement in India) first proposed the idea of a Constituent Assembly for India.
1935
Official Demand by INC: The Indian National Congress officially demanded a Constituent Assembly to frame the Constitution of India.
1938
Adult Franchise Declaration: Jawaharlal Nehru, on behalf of INC, declared: "The Constitution of free India must be framed without outside interference, by a Constituent Assembly elected on the basis of adult franchise."

🇬🇧 British Offers (WWII Era: 1939–1945)

REASON FOR OFFERS: SEEKING INDIAN COOPERATION IN WWII
  • August Offer (1940): First British recognition of Indian right to frame constitution.
  • Cripps Proposal (1942): Headed by Sir Stafford Cripps. Offered Dominion Status post-war.
  • Wavell Plan (1945): Shimla Conference discussions.
💡 Mahatma Gandhi on Cripps Proposal: Described it as a "post-dated cheque on a crashing bank."
❌ Reason for Rejection: Congress demanded Purna Swaraj (Complete Independence), whereas the Britishers only offered Dominion Status.

🏛️ Cabinet Mission Plan (1946) — The Accepted Foundation

Arrived in March 1946 with 3 British Cabinet Ministers:

1. Lord Pethick-Lawrence (Chairman) 2. Sir Stafford Cripps 3. A. V. Alexander
✅ Accepted by both the Congress and the Muslim League. Hence, the Constituent Assembly was constituted under this plan in November 1946.
🧠 MEMORY TRICK: Cabinet Mission Members
Remember the acronym "PAL": Pethick-Lawrence (Chairman), A.V. Alexander, Lord Stafford Cripps!

🏢 First Meeting & Dual Nature of the Assembly

📅 First Meeting: 9th December 1946 (Attended by 211 / 207 members). Muslim League boycotted.
  • Temporary President: Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha (Oldest member; followed French practice).
  • Permanent President: Dr. Rajendra Prasad (Elected on 11 Dec 1946).
  • Two Vice Presidents: H. C. Mukherjee and V. T. Krishnamachari.
  • Constitutional Advisor: Sir B. N. Rau.
  • Chief Draftsman: S. N. Mukherjee.
  • Secretary: H. V. R. Iyengar.
⚙️ Dual Functions:
• When meeting as Constituent Body (to frame Constitution) ➔ Chaired by Dr. Rajendra Prasad.
• When meeting as Legislative Body (India's 1st Parliament) ➔ Chaired by G. V. Mavalankar.

📜 The Historic Objective Resolution

FOUNDATION OF PREAMBLE

Moved by Jawaharlal Nehru on 13th December 1946; unanimously adopted on 22nd January 1947.

"This Constituent Assembly declares its firm and solemn resolve to proclaim India as an Independent Sovereign Republic and to draw up for her future governance a Constitution..."
  • Declared territories of British India + Indian States + other willing parts shall form a Union.
  • Stipulated that all power and authority of sovereign independent India are derived from the people.
  • Its modified version forms the Preamble of the modern Constitution.

✒️ The Drafting Committee (Set up: 29 Aug 1947)

7 MEMBERS • SAT FOR 141 DAYS • 1ST MEETING: 30 AUG 1947
  • 1. Dr. B. R. Ambedkar (Chairman) — Celebrated as "Modern Manu" or "Father of Indian Constitution".
  • 2. K. M. Munshi
  • 3. Muhammad Saadullah
  • 4. Alladi Krishnaswami Ayyar
  • 5. N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar
  • 6. B. L. Mitter ➔ Resigned due to ill-health, replaced by N. Madhav Rau.

📖 Drafts & Three Readings of Constitution

Drafts Published:

  • 1st Draft (21 Feb 1948): Given 8 months for public scrutiny and feedback.
  • 2nd Draft (21 Oct 1948): Incorporated feedback.
  • Final Draft (4 Nov 1948): Formally introduced by Dr. Ambedkar.

Three Official Readings:

ReadingDurationSignificance
1st Reading4 Nov – 9 Nov 1948 (5 days)General discussion on provisions
2nd Reading15 Nov 1948 – 17 Oct 1949 (11 mos, 2 days)Clause by clause examination & amendments
3rd Reading14 Nov – 26 Nov 1949Motion passed & Constitution adopted on 26 Nov 1949

📅 Other Historic Functions of the Assembly

  • National Flag Adopted: 22 July 1947. Designed by Pingali Venkayya (Ratio 3:2, approved by Gandhi in Vijayawada 1921).
  • Commonwealth Membership: Ratified in May 1949.
  • National Anthem & National Song: Adopted on 24 January 1950 ("Jana Gana Mana" by Rabindranath Tagore; "Vande Mataram" by Bankim Chandra Chatterjee).
  • First President of India Elected: Dr. Rajendra Prasad on 24 Jan 1950 (took office 26 Jan 1950).
  • Provisions in force on 26 Nov 1949: Citizenship (Articles 5–9), Elections (Articles 324–329), Provisional Parliament.
  • Republic Day Selection: 26 January was chosen because in 1930, Purna Swaraj Day was celebrated on this date following the 1929 Lahore Congress Session.

⚡ Commonly Confused Facts (Must Avoid In Exam)

26 November 1949 (Adoption Date)

Constitution was adopted, enacted, and given to ourselves. Celebrated as Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas). Provisions on Citizenship, Elections, and Provisional Parliament commenced.

26 January 1950 (Enforcement Date)

Full commencement of the Constitution. Celebrated as Republic Day. Chosen to commemorate 26 Jan 1930 (Purna Swaraj Day) of the 1929 Lahore Session.

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

Chairman of the Drafting Committee (7 members). Father of the Indian Constitution / "Modern Manu".

Sir B. N. Rau

Constitutional Advisor to the Constituent Assembly. Prepared the initial base draft before the Drafting Committee scrutinized it.

Dual Role: Dr. Rajendra Prasad

Chaired the Assembly whenever it met as a Constituent Body (to frame the Constitution).

Dual Role: G. V. Mavalankar

Chaired the Assembly whenever it met as a Legislative Body (India's First Provisional Parliament).
