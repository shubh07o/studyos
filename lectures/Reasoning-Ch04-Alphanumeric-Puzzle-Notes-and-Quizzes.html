System Graph 1 • Direction Flowchart

Positional Arithmetic Decision Graph

Same = Subtract (–) | Opposite = Add (+)
Flowchart: How to Calculate Relative Position in 2 Seconds
CASE A: IDENTICAL ENDS SUBTRACT (m – n)

When both direction references are identical, net displacement is reduced:

• Left of Left = (m – n) from the LEFT end
• Right of Right = (m – n) from the RIGHT end
Example: 5th to the right of 17th from the right = (17 – 5) = 12th from Right end.
CASE B: OPPOSITE ENDS ADD (m + n)

When moving away from the reference point toward the opposite side, displacements accumulate:

• Right of Left = (m + n) from the LEFT end
• Left of Right = (m + n) from the RIGHT end
Example: 4th to the left of 9th from the right = (9 + 4) = 13th from Right end.
❝

"In English phrasing, always focus on the anchor preposition: 'nth to the right OF mth from the LEFT'. The end specified after 'FROM THE' gives your anchor base, and you move away from it. Never count manually term-by-term!"

System Graph 2 • Sequence Architecture

"Preceded By" vs "Followed By" Structural Blueprint

1. PRECEEDED BY (BEFORE / LEFT)
[ PRECEDER ] ➔ [ TARGET ]

"Target is immediately preceded by Preceder" means the Preceder lies directly to the LEFT of the target.

2. FOLLOWED BY (AFTER / RIGHT)
[ TARGET ] ➔ [ FOLLOWER ]

"Target is immediately followed by Follower" means the Follower lies directly to the RIGHT of the target.

3. SANDWICHED / FLANKED
[ BEFORE ] ➔ [ TARGET ] ➔ [ AFTER ]

"Target is preceded by X and followed by Y" creates a 3-element unbroken triplet: [ X ] - [ TARGET ] - [ Y ].

⚠️

Examiner Trap Alert: Prime Number Definitions

Always remember: 2 is the only even prime number. 1 is NEITHER prime NOR composite. When the question asks for "prime followed by even", pairs like 2–4, 2–6, 5–8, 7–2, 3–6 are valid!
