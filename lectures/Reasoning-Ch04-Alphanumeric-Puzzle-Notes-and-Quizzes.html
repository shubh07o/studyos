Fundamental formulas, element terminology, and active recall drills.

🧭 1. Left-Right Direction Arithmetic

When finding an element located at a certain position relative to another element in a sequence:

Same Directions (Subtract) • Left of Left = (m - n) from Left end
• Right of Right = (m - n) from Right end
Example: 5th to the right of 17th from the right = (17 - 5) = 12th from Right.
Opposite Directions (Add) • Right of Left = (m + n) from Left end
• Left of Right = (m + n) from Right end
Example: 6th to the left of 14th from the left = (14 - 6) = 8th from Left.
Golden Direction Rule: In English phrasing, look at the reference preposition: "nth to the right OF mth from the LEFT" means start at the LEFT end and move (m + n) positions!
🔤 2. "Preceded By" vs "Followed By" vs "In Between"

Examiners frequently invert these conditions to test comprehension speed:

Preceded By (Before / Left) "A is preceded by B"
Pattern: [ B ] → [ A ]
B comes immediately to the LEFT of A.
Followed By (After / Right) "A is followed by B"
Pattern: [ A ] → [ B ]
B comes immediately to the RIGHT of A.
Between / Sandwich "A is preceded by B and followed by C"
Pattern: [ B ] → [ A ] → [ C ]
A is strictly flanked between B and C.
⚡ Interactive Drills: Test Yourself Before Quizzes
Drill 1: In string "9 4 7 2 6 8 3 5", find the 3rd to the right of 7th from the left.
Formula: Opposite directions (Right of Left) = (7 + 3) = 10th element from Left end.
Sequence has 8 elements. (10th is beyond length, or with wrap-around, strictly verify bounds!).
Drill 2: Which is the ONLY even prime number in mathematics?
Answer: 2. All other prime numbers (3, 5, 7, 11, 13...) are odd. Remember: 1 is neither prime nor composite!
Drill 3: In a series if all symbols are dropped, what happens to relative element numbering?
Rule: Do NOT rewrite the string. Simply count only letters and numbers, ignoring symbols on the fly to save 40+ seconds!

Standard Resultant Rules in RRB / SSC:
• Step 1: Even followed by Odd → Addition (Even + Odd).
• Step 2: Odd followed by Perfect Square → Difference (|Square - Odd|).
• Step 3: Odd followed by another Odd (not square) → Addition (Odd + Odd).
• Step 4: Odd followed by Even (not square) → Multiplication (Odd × Even).
• Step 5: Even followed by another Even → Division (First ÷ Second).
