Golden English Grammar Rules & Root Word Vault

All high-yield formulas, fixed prepositions, pronoun rules, and phobia/phile root words covered in the 30 PYQs.

1. Subject-Verb Agreement Rules (Q1, Q5, Q25)

  • Do / Does / Did + Base Verb (V1): She / He / It के साथ doesn't लगता है और उसके बाद मुख्य क्रिया में s/es नहीं लगता।
    ✔ She doesn't like...  |  ✘ She doesn't likes...
  • Each of / Either of / Neither of / One of: Formula: Each / Neither of + Plural Noun + Singular Verb
    • Each of the students has passed. (Q5)
    • Neither of the boys was present. (Q25)

2. Fixed Prepositions Master List (Q10, Q16, Q22, Q27, Q28)

Latin Adjectives & Prefer (+ TO, never THAN):
Senior Junior Superior Inferior Prior Prefer Preferable के बाद हमेशा to आता है, और इनके पहले more / most नहीं लगता।
✔ I prefer tea to coffee.  |  ✔ He is senior to me.
Insist + on (+ V-ing)
He insisted on paying.
Fond + of (शौकीन होना)
I am fond of reading.
Divide + into (भागों में)
Divided into 5 chapters.
Good in / Bad at
Very good in Mathematics.

3. Root Words: Phobias (डर) & Philia (प्यार) (Q4, Q18)

Bibliophile: पुस्तकों से प्यार (Biblio = Book + Phile = Love)
Hydrophobia: पानी का डर (Hydro = Water)
Claustrophobia: बंद या छोटी जगहों का डर
Xenophobia: अजनबियों / विदेशियों से डर
Acrophobia: ऊँचाई (High places) का डर
Pyrophobia: आग (Fire) का डर

4. Pronouns, Time, Subjunctive & Passive (Q7, Q13, Q14, Q17, Q19)

  • Pronoun Rule 2-3-1 + Subjective Case (Q14): सामान्य वाक्यों में 2nd Person → 3rd Person → 1st Person और Verb से पहले Subjective Case (I, न कि Me): "John (3) and I (1) went to the market."
  • Since vs For & On Time (Q7, Q13): Since = Point of time (2018, Monday). For = Period of time (2 hours). On time = निर्धारित समय पर (Punctual).
  • Imaginary Condition (Q17) & Active/Passive V2 (Q19): काल्पनिक वाक्यों में हमेशा If I were... आता है। Active Past Simple (V2) का Passive was/were + V3 होता है।
