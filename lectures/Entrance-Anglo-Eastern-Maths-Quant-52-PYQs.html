      📐 Trigonometry Golden Identities (Anand Sir)

      • • Complementary Rule: यदि B + C = 90°, तो sin B = cos C और tan B · tan C = 1
      • • Fundamental Identity: sin2θ + cos2θ = 1 एवं (1 − cos2θ) csc2θ = 1
      • • Range of Sine/Cosine: −1 ≤ sin θ, cos θ ≤ 1 (इसलिए sin θ · cos θ = 1 असंभव है क्योंकि sin 2θ = 2 नहीं हो सकता)।

      📏 Coordinate Geometry & Straight Lines

      • • Parallel Lines (No Intersection): a1a2 = b1b2 ≠ c1c2
      • • Slope-Intercept Form: y = mx + c जहाँ m = tan θ
      • • Intercept Form: xa + yb = 1 (Y-अक्ष पर x = 0 ⇒ (0, b))

      📦 Algebra, Polynomials & Mensuration

      • • Reciprocal Roots: द्विघात समीकरण ax2 + bx + c = 0 में व्युत्क्रम मूल होने पर c = a (अर्थात् k = 5)।
      • • Cuboid Volume from Adjacent Faces: V = √A1 · A2 · A3
      • • Complex Multiplicative Inverse: (a − bi)−1 = a + bia2 + b2

      ⚡ Quantitative Aptitude Shortcuts (Gautam Sir)

      • • 2-Year CI & SI Difference: Diff = P × (R100)2
      • • Boat & Stream: नाव की चाल B = D + U2, धारा की चाल C = D − U2
      • • Garrison / Provision Rule: M1 × D1 = M2 × D2
      • • HCF & LCM Formula: First No. × Second No. = HCF × LCM
